# Replying to responses

Draft the reply for a human to send. Keep under 100 words (`--kind reply`).

| They say | Reply approach |
|---|---|
| "Not interested" / "Unsubscribe" | One line thanking them, confirm removal, nothing else. End the sequence and record the opt-out. |
| "Not now, try later" | Ask for a rough time ("Q1?"), confirm you will reach out then, no pitch. |
| "We already have a vendor" | Do not argue. Ask whether they would like an independent view of the specific exposure you noticed, or leave it. |
| "Send more info" | Send one concrete item tied to the original signal and propose a 15-minute call. Not a brochure. |
| "How did you find this?" | Be transparent: it came from publicly visible internet data; offer to share exactly what was observed. |
| "Wrong person" | Thank them, ask who owns it, keep it to two lines. |
| Hostile or legal threat | Do not draft a persuasive reply. Flag `needs_human` and stop the sequence. |

Never reply with claims that are not in the account data, and never pressure.
