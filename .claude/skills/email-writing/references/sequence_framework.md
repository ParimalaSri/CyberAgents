# Follow-up sequence framework

A sequence is 3 to 4 short touches over about 2 weeks. Each touch adds something new; none
says "just checking in". Stop on any reply. Words are limits for `lint_email.py --kind`.

| Touch | Day | Kind | Angle | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | `first` | The observation from the data and why it matters | 120 |
| 2 | 3 | `followup` | A second fact or a one-line useful insight, not a reminder | 80 |
| 3 | 7 | `followup` | Offer something concrete (a short list of what we see, no strings) | 80 |
| 4 | 14 | `breakup` | Polite close: "I'll stop here; reply if timing changes" | 50 |

## Rules
- Every touch cites something factual from the account data (`signals_used`). Reuse a signal
  only with a new angle.
- One question per email. Make it easy to answer with a word.
- Never imply the previous email was ignored or that the reader is negligent.
- Same thread (reply to the first subject) for touches 2 and 3; new subject for touch 4 only if it helps.
- Respect opt-outs: any "no", "unsubscribe" or "not interested" ends the sequence and is recorded.
- Send times, volume and consent rules are the sender's responsibility; this skill drafts text only.
